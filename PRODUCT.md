# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Security recruiters and hiring managers, security research peers, collaborators, and prospective clients. They may arrive on desktop or mobile and need to understand the owner's technical credibility, work, and way of thinking without friction.

## Product Purpose

Present HacksonAloysis as a trustworthy security-engineering portfolio. Show concrete work and evidence, make the owner's professional story easy to follow, and make contact straightforward. Success means a visitor can quickly understand the work, distinguish public projects from private commercial work, and find a clear contact path.

## Brand Personality

Evidence-led, editorial, and technically assured. Curious, precise, and composed.

## Anti-references

- Generic neon "hacker" aesthetics or stock cyber imagery.
- Template-like SaaS dashboards and repetitive card grids.
- Unsupported technical claims or presentation of private commercial work as public evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Show evidence before making claims; label what is public, private, or unavailable.
2. Preserve the archive and case-file visual identity while making content legible and usable on small screens.
3. Keep navigation, project context, and contact actions easy to find.
4. Treat motion as expressive enhancement, never as a prerequisite for understanding or operating the site.
5. Do not expose private commercial work or publish external project material without approval.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation and visible focus, readable contrast and zoom/reflow, meaningful image alternatives, semantic structure, and reduced-motion preferences. Mobile layouts must retain access to all core content and controls without relying on hover, fine pointer input, or device-specific user-agent detection.
